Datenschutz / Privacy

This site sets no cookies, uses no advertising trackers and loads no external fonts or CDNs. The tools run locally in your browser; your inputs are never transmitted or stored.

Visitor statistics (GoatCounter). To count page views we use GoatCounter (goatcounter.com). The small counting script is served from this site. On each page view it sends the page path, page title, referrer, screen size, browser user agent and language to forgeagentops.goatcounter.com; the country is derived from your IP address, but the IP address itself is not stored. GoatCounter sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and builds no user profiles; a pseudonymous hash to count unique visits is discarded after at most 8 hours. Inputs into the tools are never sent. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in aggregate, anonymous reach statistics). You can object at any time, e.g. by blocking the script with a content blocker. Details: GoatCounter privacy policy.
